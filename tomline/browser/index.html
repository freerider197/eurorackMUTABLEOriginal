Browser model of the Tomline firmware for Streams. The sound comes from a bit-exact JavaScript port of the firmware's voice.cc and parameters.cc, with the module's controls as the firmware reads them. Try patches and demos here first, then on the module.

The voice and knob maths give the same VCA and filter words as the firmware, sample for sample: tomline/test/compare_browser.js checks this against the C++ code. The analog part (DAC filter, VCA, low-pass, output) follows the Streams v05 schematic with nominal part values, so levels and cutoff frequencies are estimates until measured on a module. The bass tom and low tom characters are modeled by ear.
